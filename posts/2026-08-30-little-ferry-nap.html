Little Ferry Nap

August 30, 2026

Took a quick little trip with a friend to Vashon Island this weekend. Matter of fact, I took a little trip with a friend to Vashon Island last weekend too. Huh. I should post about that one.

While we were on the ferry on our way there, I looked to my right and saw the cutest thing. A giant pitbull trying to figure out how to climb over the center console of the front seat of the neighboring car so that it could lay down on it's mama's lap and take a nap. And after much anxiety and trial and error, the pooch finally figured it out. And I loved the way it was laying down all awkward, butt in lap and chin on open window.

I immediately thought that it would make such cute painting, but I was worried about taking a picture and looking creepy. But I moved fast and snapped a pic, and last night I did a quick painting of it in Procreate

I don't typically love paintings like that one, they're just not my style. But I wanted to capture the moment and I'm glad I did.
