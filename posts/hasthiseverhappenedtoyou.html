Has This Ever Happened to You?

I have this problem recently. I’ve been incredibly inspired to create and learn new things that it’s sort of dominating my brain space. I’ll be honest, I haven’t had this kind of spark to create in a very long time, and never at this level. It’s coming from deep inside, and it’s literally keeping me up at night. There’s so much I want to do and learn that I can’t turn my brain off. I’ll be exhausted at the end of the day and I’ll just be laying there in bed unable to sleep, my brain spinning.

I’ve never been pulled towards one kind of artistic expression, I’ve always been excited by many things. Drawing is one of those things, especially portraits. I’m working on one right now. It’s been 12 hours of work so far and I’m not anywhere near done. I think about the drawing all the time. It takes serious will power to put it away and come back to it later. But I can feel the portrait sitting there staring at me. Thumping sounds coming from the drawing pad like Jumanji.

I’m building this website too. There’s still a lot of work to be done. I’m drawing the image assets, learning css, learning GitHub and Terminal command prompt, and I’m loving every minute of it. When I’m not drawing I’m working on the site, slowly figuring out the code and making it look the way I want.

I’m writing story ideas down all the time. Little blurbs about characters; heroes and villains, settings and worlds. All of these things to hopefully one day put down on a page or in film.

I’m filming little moments with my iPhone, using Cinematic mode at 24fps usually, to hopefully create cohesive videos to post. I love little videos. Here’s one I made that I love.

I have all of these feelings, intense urges to bring things in my mind to life. My mind is just going going going, even when I’m exhausted.

I’m grateful for it, but I am worried about burn out. It’s happened before. I don’t know if it’s because of ADHD or whatever my version of non-neuro-typical-ness is, but I tend to obsess over things. The fire burns hot and fast and then I’m burnt out. I don’t want that to happen. I want to take my projects slowly and give myself the time and space to learn things properly. I'm taking this current bout of inspiration much slower than I would have in the past, so I'm hopeful that I can maintain it in a steady way, rather than burning out. I’m trying to be mindful of my energy and not overdo it. I’m trying to take breaks and rest when I need to. But it’s hard. The pull is strong.

Is this normal?
