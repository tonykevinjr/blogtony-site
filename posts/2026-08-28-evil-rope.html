Evil Rope

August 28, 2026

The biggest hurdle for me on creating this blog so far has been writing the blog posts. I wrote one weeks ago just for the sake of having filler while I build out this website. I'm not a fan of it. I don't like how it reads or feels while you read it. It's not clean or polished, and it feels like a kid wrote it. You can tell I rushed my way through it.

The mere fact that I had to write it at all was annoying and writing itself was a chore. And while I doubt that anyone will ever see it because its going to get buried under a pile of posts and, well, who's reading this blog anyway... I still hate that it exists in the form that it does. It's lazy and roughshod and sloppy, like a house kept together with scotch tape. Its a living reminder of this part of my creative self that I'm learning to see for the first time. And it's a scary monster.

But that's been the story of my creative life in a nutshell, really.

  1. Feel inspired
  2. Start a project
  3. Get anxious
  4. Crank it out
  5. Stick tape on it
  6. Show it to the world for validation as fast as possible
  7. Quickly move on

That's the thing. Anxiety.

Looking back on all of the artistic eras of my life, one of the throughlines through all of them has been an anxious need to be done with the projects I start. Yes, I also had the "need" to start the projects; inspiration happens and heats up fast and one must strike while the iron is hot. But once I'm locked in to a project, my body starts to feel an intense need to get it done. I used to think this was so I could take a picture of the finished product and get validation from people online, and that does still feel good, but now I know that its because of something else that uses my anxiety.

I know it has a name, but I can't remmeber it. Something that starts with an "R." This feeling of, I don't know what, a thick snake-like rope, like three inches in diameter pulling from inside my body towards the end of the project. The rope is attached to my gut, loops upwards, and comes out between my eyes. It's a pulsing, off-white, living, glowing tube and it's angry and slippery. I really don't like it so I have to finish the project so that it goes slack and slinks back inside my body.

Is that weird?

Anyway, how is that related to this blog? Huh. I honestly have no idea. I think maybe the thought was that the idea of building the site is much less scary than writing the blog posts themselves. Because that big rope, snake, whatever monster is going to come out and start pulling, and I have to somehow figure out how to fight it, take my time, enjoy that slow process, and create.

Oh that's right, it's called Resistance.

I have a feeling Resistance will be a common topic here.
