Hey Pal. If you're seeing this its cuz I like ya and want your opinion on this site as I build it. It's technically live but no one really knows about it. A couple things to note:

  1. It looks better on a Desktop, please explore on your computer. There are animations and easter eggs and shit. Click everything and see what happens. Note: Most eggs are only visible in Neon Mode
  2. It has a Matrix theme because when I was 12 I made a shitty Geocities site that was Matrix themed. That movie was my entire personality back then. I'm trying to honor little Tony with this and hopefully heal a tiny little part of my inner child
  3. The blog posts can be found on the "Posts" page, or under the "Recent Posts" portion of the sidebar
  4. This is very much a work in progress, but I'm proud of it so far. Please give feedback!
  5. I'm SUPER discouraged by the current state of social media, while also really missing the golden age of the internet from 25 years ago. If you want to join me in this fun attempt at taking back control of our own online presence, join me on Neocities!
  6. Oh and also. If the Neon-ness of the site hurts your eyes, switch to "Soft" mode by clicking "MODE" at the top of the page. Soft mode is boring and there are no animations or easter eggs, but it's oh so soft, wow

Love ya, Pal.
