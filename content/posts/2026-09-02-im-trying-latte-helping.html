Some days you wake up and you just feel so blah. I say "you" but I mean "me." I don't know why I put "me" in quotes. Fuck, I keep doing it. "I" need to re-learn my grammar.

I used to work in an office, see. I used to make beaucoup bucks (did you know that was spelled "beaucoup?" Fuck I did it again) in my highfalutin tech job career. Then the layoff happened. And. Now I work at a restaurant.

A restaurant that I love, by the way. I adore then owners and the staff. It's just... sometimes I really don't like that I work in a kitchen and I get super discouraged. I feel sort of stuck. Cue Freddie Mercury desiring to break free via song.

But on the (Mr.?) brightside, I made such a bomb latte today you guys, it's not even funny. The perfect espresso pull with the silkiest foamy oat milk. I can't do latte art for shit, but I don't care. I made noises upon each sip. My sister made my mug too, so that probably definitely maybe helps enhance the flavor.

Today will be a good day.

It has to be a good day. It may be rainy and yucky outside, but "my" (sigh) latte has transformed the outlook. And the weather man says "mostly latte with a 100% chance of joy."
